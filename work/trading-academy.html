Built in 2026. The repository is private and the site is pre-launch; taking payments is the next piece of work.

The product

A course platform for students, teachers and admins, with two written courses: a free foundations course and a paid course. Between them there are 19 lessons, 68 graphics and 12 Excel workbooks. Alongside the courses is a trading journal, sold as a paid feature.

The journal

  • A five-step quick log, screenshots, a dashboard built around three questions a trader actually asks, weekly reviews, a psychology tracker, a bias review and a backtest mode.
  • CSV import and export. Reading and exporting your own data is never locked, even when your access lapses.
  • A free tier (20 trades by default, set by an admin), then paid access through an entitlements table, with an admin screen to unlock it by hand until payments exist.
  • Journal data is private to its owner. Admins and teachers can't read it, and the database enforces that, not the UI.

Access the database decides

  • Paid lesson text lives only in the database and is served only to enrolled students. A production build contains none of the demo data or paid text.
  • 48 access-rule and account-deletion checks run every migration against a real Postgres, in CI.
  • Admin actions that need elevated rights, like inviting and deleting users, run in a Supabase Edge Function rather than the browser.

Quality

  • WCAG 2.1 AA checks with axe in end-to-end tests, in light and dark mode, at phone and desktop width.
  • End-to-end journeys for students, teachers and admins.
  • A reviewed course: maths checked, facts checked and a 73-point lesson audit.
